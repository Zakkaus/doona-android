# Brand marks in `res/drawable/rv_brand_*.xml`

Brand glyphs for the service rows of 新增服務規則 (`RvServiceRulesPage`). Every mark remains its owner's trademark and
is used only to name that owner's service.

## Simple Icons

From
[Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) **16.38.1** (npm `simple-icons@16.38.1`, `icons/<slug>.svg`), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 asks for no notice; this file keeps the provenance.
None of the icons below carries its own licence in the package's `data/simple-icons.json`. The marks remain their
owners' trademarks; Simple Icons' `DISCLAIMER.md` applies.

Each drawable is the SVG's one path, unchanged except that its numbers are written with separators; the 24-unit glyph
sits in a 28.8-unit viewport (20 dp of the 24 dp box, as `rv_sym_github` and the Material Symbols glyphs), filled
white and tinted by the row. Generated by `tools/gen_brand_icons.py` from the npm tarball
(`https://registry.npmjs.org/simple-icons/-/simple-icons-16.38.1.tgz`).

## How they are drawn

`tools/gen_brand_marks.py` keeps each file's paths (numbers re-emitted with separators) and scales the mark so its
longer side is 20 of the 24 units, centred: 20 dp of the 24 dp box, as the Simple Icons marks. A wide wordmark (Prime
Video, Nintendo, Disney+, Blizzard) is 20 dp wide and shorter. Each drawable was rendered against its source at 192 px
with rsvg-convert: no pixel differs by more than 8/255.
